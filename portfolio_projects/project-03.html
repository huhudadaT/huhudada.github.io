§ Overview

About this project.

A laboratory coursework project focused on isolating and characterizing the SV40 OBD protein, which initiates viral DNA replication by binding to the viral origin of replication.

§ Approach

Methods & process.

Standard protein purification techniques were used to isolate the target protein from a bacterial expression system. Purity and identity were confirmed using gel electrophoresis.

§ Outcomes

Results.

The exercise reinforced practical skills in protein biochemistry and provided a working understanding of viral replication initiation. Detailed results to be added.
