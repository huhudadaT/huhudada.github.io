Title: The French Revolution
Description: Key dates, figures, and causes of the French Revolution.
Subject: History
Semester: Spring 2026

Q: In what year was the Estates-General convened, opening the revolutionary crisis?
A. 1787
B. 1788
*C. 1789
D. 1791
Explain: Louis XVI convened the Estates-General in May 1789, the first time since 1614.

Q: Which group declared itself the National Assembly in June 1789?
*A. The Third Estate
B. The Parlement of Paris
C. The Paris Commune
D. The Directory
Explain: Third Estate deputies renamed themselves the National Assembly after being refused a joint vote.

Q: What condition of the royal treasury forced Louis XVI to call the Estates-General?
A. A British trade embargo
*B. Bankruptcy from war debt and deficits
C. A collapse in colonial silver
D. A tax strike by the peasantry
Explain: Debt from the American war left the crown unable to borrow, so new taxes required the Estates-General.

Q: The Reign of Terror was directed chiefly by which body?
A. The Legislative Assembly
B. The Girondin faction
C. The Parlement of Paris
*D. The Committee of Public Safety
Explain: The Committee of Public Safety, dominated by Robespierre, ran the Terror from 1793 to 1794.
