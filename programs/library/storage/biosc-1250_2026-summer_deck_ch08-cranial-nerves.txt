Name: BIOSC 1250 Ch08 Cranial Nerves
Description: The twelve cranial nerves, their names, modalities, and functions.
Class: BIOSC 1250
Semester: Summer 2026

Q: Cranial nerve I
A: Olfactory
Sensory. Carries smell from the nasal epithelium.

Q: Cranial nerve II
A: Optic
Sensory. Carries vision from the retina.

Q: Cranial nerve III
A: Oculomotor
Motor. Moves the eye, constricts the pupil, adjusts lens shape, and raises the eyelid.

Q: Cranial nerve IV
A: Trochlear
Motor. Moves the eye via the superior oblique muscle (down and outward).

Q: Cranial nerve V
A: Trigeminal
Mixed. Sensation from the face via three branches, and controls chewing.

Q: Cranial nerve VI
A: Abducens
Motor. Moves the eye laterally via the lateral rectus muscle.

Q: Cranial nerve VII
A: Facial
Mixed. Facial expression, taste on the anterior tongue, salivation, and tearing.

Q: Cranial nerve VIII
A: Vestibulocochlear
Sensory. Hearing and balance.

Q: Cranial nerve IX
A: Glossopharyngeal
Mixed. Taste on the posterior tongue, swallowing, and salivation.

Q: Cranial nerve X
A: Vagus
Mixed. Parasympathetic control of the viscera, plus swallowing and speech.

Q: Cranial nerve XI
A: Accessory
Motor. Controls the neck and shoulder muscles.

Q: Cranial nerve XII
A: Hypoglossal
Motor. Controls tongue movement.
